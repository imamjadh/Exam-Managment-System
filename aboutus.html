Advanced Tecnological Institute - Nawalapitiya

Welcome to the Exam Management System of ATI Nawalapitiya - SLIATE. This system is developed as part of our academic project under the Higher National Diploma in Information Technology (HNDIT) program. Our goal is to simplify and streamline the exam-related processes within our ATI. With this system, we aim to reduce manual workload, improve accuracy, and provide an efficient platform for managing exams.

Our Mission

To support the academic success of students and staff by providing a reliable, easy-to-use digital solution for exam administration.

Our Team

We are a group of passionate HNDIT students from ATI Nawalapitiya, committed to using technology to solve real-world problems in education.
